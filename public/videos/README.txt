Place your licensed construction video here and name it:

  construction.mp4

Recommended video:
- MP4 (H.264), 1920x1080 or 1280x720
- 10–25 seconds, seamless loop if possible
- No audio (the page video is muted)
- Optimized for web; ideally under 15 MB
- Use footage you own or have permission to use

The page will show /images/construction-poster.svg until the video is added.
You can replace the poster SVG with a project render or approved construction still.
